Privacy policy

Last updated: 13 October 2026

This policy explains how Shri Laxmi Silver Palace (“we”, “us”) handles information when you use the LSP catalogue mobile application. The app lets you browse silver jewellery, save pieces on your device, and contact our team. It does not place orders or take payments.

Information you provide

The catalogue app does not ask you to create a customer account, and it does not collect your name, address, phone number, precise location, payment details, or contacts. Saved pieces are stored on your device and are not sent to us.

If you choose to contact us using WhatsApp, that link opens WhatsApp. Any message and account information you share there are handled by WhatsApp under its own privacy policy.

Catalogue and technical requests

The app requests product names, descriptions, category information, prices where available, and product photographs from our hosted catalogue service so it can display the collection. Our database and hosting providers process the network requests needed to deliver those pages and images. We do not use the app to profile customers, serve advertisements, or track browsing activity.

How we use information

We use the catalogue information to show the collection and keep product details current. We do not sell customer information. The owner’s separate catalogue administration portal uses an authenticated account to maintain product and category records; those sign-in details are not required to browse the mobile app.

Storage and retention

Your saved-piece list remains on your device until you remove pieces or clear the app’s local data. Product catalogue information is kept while it is part of our active collection. Technical request logs, if retained by our hosting providers, follow those providers’ settings and retention periods.

Children

The app is a general silver catalogue and is not designed to collect information from children. It does not include child-directed accounts or features.

Your choices

You can remove a saved piece in the app or clear its local storage by uninstalling it. You can choose not to open WhatsApp. You can still browse the catalogue without sending us personal information.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy when the app or our services change. The latest version will be available at this page. For privacy questions, contact us on WhatsApp or email [ADD BUSINESS CONTACT EMAIL].
